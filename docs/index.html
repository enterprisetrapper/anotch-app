Download for Mac

Free and open source · Apple silicon · macOS 15+

What you can do with Anotch

Ask about your screen

Attach a screenshot or watch a selected window or display for new questions. Apple Vision OCR reads screen text locally.

Transcribe Mac audio

Listen to your microphone, Mac audio, or both. Ask about the transcript, recap decisions, or export it as Markdown.

Pick how much to read

Use Direct for a short answer, Concise for the key detail, or Full for the reasoning. Direct can give just the letter for multiple-choice questions.

Choose your AI provider

Use Apple Intelligence for on-device answers on a supported Mac running macOS 26 or later, or connect OpenRouter or another OpenAI-compatible provider.

Connected providers receive the context you send. Anotch stores API keys using macOS Keychain-backed encryption.

Install Anotch on macOS

  1. Download the DMG and drag Anotch into Applications.
  2. Open Anotch. This preview ships without Apple notarization. If macOS blocks the first attempt, use Open Anyway in System Settings under Privacy & Security.
  3. Click the notch to open Anotch, then pick an answer provider in Settings.
